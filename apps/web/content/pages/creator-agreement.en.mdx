## Introduction

This Creator Agreement (the "Agreement") is between you and Tianjin Julians Technology Co., Ltd. (the "Platform" / "we"). ## 1. Definitions

**Platform** — our marketplace for creation, submission, review, listing, distribution, management, community interaction, transaction matching and support.

**Creator / you** — a natural person or entity registered as a creator.

**End user** — anyone who obtains, installs, subscribes, invokes, rates or uses assets.

**Assets** — capability units you develop, configure, submit or publish for agents/users, including Skills, MCP servers, A2A agents, workflows, personas, tools, prompts, templates and similar forms. "Skill" and "asset" are used interchangeably here. Official/first-party labelled capabilities are excluded and follow separate rules.

**Free / paid assets** — as configured by you regarding whether payment is required.

**Asset materials** — code, prompts, SKILL.md, docs, media, samples and other materials you submit.

**Non-guaranteed transaction model** — users pay your payout channel directly; the Platform does not pool funds, collect-and-pay, or take a platform service fee/commission from those asset payments under the current model. Channel fees charged by WeChat/Alipay remain yours under your agreement with them. Any future collect-and-settle or commission model requires your clear agreement via supplement or posted rules.

**Payout channel** — your WeChat Pay merchant account (company) or Alipay metered individual payout, bound per Platform rules, matching your verified subject.

## 2. Account, KYC and payout binding

Complete registration, real-name/entity verification and other required steps. Keep information accurate and update within three (3) business days of changes. Obtain any sector licences before listing. Do not transfer or lend your creator account. Notify us of compromise and rotate secrets promptly.

Paid listing requires a bound payout channel whose subject matches your KYC, with valid payment-service agreements and no freezes/restrictions that block normal settlement. If binding fails, we may suspend paid listing until remedied; prior orders/refunds/invoices remain your duty. You must cooperate with AML/KYC and risk checks.

You may list free or paid assets (or open-source where supported) if you own or are licensed to distribute them. The Platform provides tooling for create/submit/review/list/distribute/trade; except for official assets, we do not author your content for you.

We may run automated scans and human review for security, data handling and compliance. **Review, filtering or listing does not constitute a warranty, guarantee, endorsement or regulatory certification** of legality, accuracy, safety, fitness or non-infringement, and does not reduce your duties.

Demo cases you supply must be truthful, non-misleading, lawfully authorised and desensitised where needed. Demos may be labelled as examples and do not promise production results.

Except for official labels, do not imply Platform authorship, certification or final responsibility.

## 4. Content rules

Comply with law, public order and Platform rules. Prohibited content includes illegal material, malware, infringement, fraud, unauthorised scraping, grey-market tooling and similar. Do not misuse Platform brands or imply false affiliation. Do not steer users off-platform for payment/data collection without permission. Generative AI, high-risk automation and regulated domains (medical, legal, finance, minors, etc.) need lawful notices and must not replace licensed professionals where law so requires. High-impact automated actions need clear prompts and human confirmation. Do not bypass the non-guaranteed model via unregistered payouts, crypto side-deals or split settlement that hides orders. No deceptive pricing or inducement to pay.

You may request delisting. Protect purchasers already entitled to use the asset (continued term, substitute, refund or other reasonable cure). Channel fees, taxes and invoicing to users are yours. You must not use proceeds for money laundering or other crimes and must cooperate with lawful investigations. Handle minor-related refund claims under PRC law. We may coordinate refunds for severe defects, material misdescription, illegality, abandonment of service or lawful orders; you still execute payout. Cooperate on dispute mediation; after-sales remain primarily yours.

You maintain assets and dependencies. Material changes affecting paid users need advance notice and reasonable cure (old version window, migrate, extend, refund, etc.). Pricing changes adverse to existing subscribers need at least fifteen (15) days' notice and a fair exit/refund path.
