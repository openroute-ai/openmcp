## Summary

Updated 11 October 2026: this update clarifies Device Code / device authorisation login, install callbacks and usage logs, aligned with paid asset trading (non-guaranteed model) and creator payout scenarios.

We comply with applicable laws and privacy principles to provide safer, more reliable services.

OpenMCP Hub is an AI asset platform operated by Tianjin Julians Technology Co., Ltd. ("we"), offering discovery, publishing, install and trading of Skills, MCP servers, A2A agents and similar assets.

Please read this Policy to understand how we process personal information. Sensitive-information topics are emphasised in bold. Agreeing to this Policy means you understand processing that is necessary for the features you use — we do **not** process every category listed merely because you open the product. We follow **data minimisation**.

This Policy applies to products and services we provide. Except as stated, it does not cover third-party products or services.

## 1. Collection and use

### 1.1 Collection scenarios

#### 1.1.1 Account registration and sign-in

We support email/password, phone OTP, and (where enabled) GitHub, Google or similar third-party login.

**(1) Email/password** — username/nickname, password and an email address in mainland China for verification and service notices. Without these you cannot register, but may still browse/search.

**(2) Phone OTP** — mobile number for codes and notices.

**(3) Third-party login** — account id, nickname, avatar and similar public fields as shown on the authorisation screen.

**Identity verification:** Where required by law or for creator paid listing / payout, **we collect real-name information (name, ID document numbers and images, validity, phone, and company KYC materials as submitted).** Refusing verification blocks those features only.

**(4) Teams** — we do not currently offer third-party IdP team spaces; if organised/team features launch, we will explain processing separately.

**(5) Device Code / client install**

When you use OpenWork, Studio, Store MCP or other supported clients with **OAuth Device Code** (or similar) to sign in and install assets, **we process device/user codes, authorisation status, client identifiers, linked account, authorisation time, token metadata needed for install/invoke, and security logs.** Device codes are one-time or short-lived and are invalidated after exchange or expiry. We do not request payment passwords or bank card numbers through this flow.

Install callbacks, entitlement checks and metering may process asset ids, install results and usage/invocation log fields needed for delivery, billing assistance, risk control and debugging — on a minimised basis.

#### 1.1.2 Search

We may collect keywords and search history (some cached locally) to provide search.

#### 1.1.3 Browse, install and creator uploads

Anonymous browse/install generally does not collect identity-linked data beyond device/IP/logs in 1.1.8. Creator uploads may include asset files, uploader IP (abuse prevention) and contact/account data for management and notices.

#### 1.1.4 Creator KYC

For paid listing / payout we may collect company or individual identity documents and payout channel identifiers (WeChat Pay merchant id / Alipay metered account). Sensitive data is processed only as needed for verification and compliance.

#### 1.1.5 Install risk notice

Some assets are third-party. Review permissions and trust the source before install.

#### 1.1.6 Paid trading

Paid trades use a **non-guaranteed model**: users pay creators directly; the Platform does not hold the funds. We process **order data** (order id, asset, time, amount, payee creator, status) and necessary logs for matching and disputes. **We do not collect payment passwords, bank card numbers or full payment credentials** — those stay with the payment channel.

#### 1.1.7 Sandbox preview

If you use sandbox/debug preview, we collect inputs you submit, asset/session metadata, status, outputs and necessary run logs. Entrusted mainland LLM providers act as processors under our instructions and must not use the data for their own unrelated purposes. **Do not submit sensitive personal information, secrets or unauthorised third-party data in sandbox.**

#### 1.1.8 Logs, devices and security

We automatically record network logs (IP, time, pages, browser/OS, device identifiers, referrers) for security, abuse prevention, debugging, analytics and improvement.

### 1.2 Use rules

We use information within the purposes stated at collection. New, unrelated purposes require fresh notice and consent where required. Legal bases that do not require consent under PRC PIPL (contract necessity, legal duties, emergencies, etc.) still apply as permitted.

### 1.3 Cookies

We may store cookies for login continuity and preferences on https://www.openmcp.cn. See the Cookie Policy. You may block cookies in the browser, which may degrade features.

## 2. Storage

We retain personal information only as long as needed for the purpose, except for legal retention. On service shutdown we will notify you and delete or anonymise data where required. Personal information collected in mainland China is stored in mainland China; we do not currently transfer it abroad.

## 3. Security

We apply encryption, access control, least-privilege staff access, audit logging and restricted KYC object storage. If a personal-information security incident occurs, we will notify you and authorities as required and contain the incident.

## 4. Sharing and disclosure

We share minimum necessary data with payment institutions, identity-verification vendors, cloud/object storage, SMS/email providers, and entrusted LLM providers for sandbox — under contracts and security requirements. We do not sell personal information. We do not publicly disclose without separate consent except as law allows. In mergers/acquisitions, successors remain bound by standards no lower than this Policy.

## 5. Your rights

Access/correct profile and (for creators) KYC/payout/order views in settings. Report abuse to service@openmcp.cn. Request account deletion in security settings; we stop processing and delete or anonymise except where law requires retention. Other PIPL rights (access, correction, deletion, withdraw consent) may be exercised via section 8; we respond within fifteen (15) days or as required by law after verifying identity.

## 6. Changes

We may revise this Policy and will highlight material updates. Using new/changed features before the stated effective date may make those updates effective for that use.

## 7. Minors

If you are under 18, obtain guardian consent before use. For children under 14, guardians should read this Policy and consent before providing information. Guardians may contact us with questions.

## 8. Contact

Personal information protection enquiries: support@openmcp.cn; infringement/reports: service@openmcp.cn; or published support channels. We aim to respond within fifteen (15) days or as required by law.
