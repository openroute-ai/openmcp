# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

OpenMCP Hub (the "platform") is a marketplace for developer assets and MCP services.
This policy explains what the platform collects when you register, publish, purchase
or otherwise use the service, why it collects it, how it is stored and protected, and
what rights you have over it.

## 1. Information we collect

**You provide it**

- **Account details:** email address, phone number, and a hash of your password. An
  account created from a phone number is given a generated email address so that it
  can sign in; nothing beyond the message you authorise is read from your SMS.
- **Provider details:** contact name, verification subject (individual or company),
  identity number, scans of your identity documents, and payout channel. These are
  used for verification and settlement, and are kept only as long as they are
  needed for those purposes.
- **Billing and payment:** order number, amount, payment status, and the WeChat or
  Alipay transaction reference. Card numbers, payment passwords and other payment
  credentials are never stored.
- **Support:** the contents of your support tickets and in-app messages.

**Collected as you use the service**

- **Security logs:** IP address, device and browser information, sign-in time, and
  captcha verification results.
- **Usage records:** the assets you view, publish, download and buy, and the request
  logs of MCP calls, used for metering, rate limiting and troubleshooting.

## 2. Why we use it

1. to create your account, sign you in and verify who you are;
2. to review provider and asset qualifications, and to pay out earnings;
3. to process orders, settlement and withdrawals;
4. to deliver in-app messages, announcements and service notices;
5. to prevent fraud and abuse, including risk control, rate limits and captchas;
6. to meet record-keeping and filing duties under applicable law.

We do not use your information for anything unrelated to the above, and we do not
sell it to third parties.

## 3. Storage and protection

- Identity documents are stored encrypted in object storage, and are accessible only
  to reviewers and the automated checks that need them.
- We keep information for as long as it is needed. On account cancellation your
  personal information is deleted or anonymised, except where the law requires us to
  retain it.
- We protect it with encryption in transit, access control and audit logging. If an
  incident affecting your rights occurs, we will tell you as required by law.

## 4. Sharing

We do not disclose your personal information to third parties other than:

- with your separate consent;
- to a partner who needs it to complete verification or payment settlement, and only
  the information that step requires;
- as required by law, a court or a competent authority;
- to a successor in a merger, acquisition or insolvency, who remains bound by this
  policy.

## 5. Your rights

You can view, correct and delete your account information from your profile settings,
and can request account cancellation. You can also ask us to access, correct or
delete your information, or to withdraw an authorisation you have given, by writing
to the address below. We verify that the request comes from the account holder before
acting on it.

## 6. Minors

The service is for adults. A minor who needs the service should read this policy with
a guardian and use it only with that guardian's consent.

## 7. Changes

If this policy changes, we will say so in-app or on the website. Continuing to use the
service means you accept the updated policy.

## 8. Contact

For any question, comment or complaint about this policy or about how your
information is handled, write to support@openmcp.cn or to the office address the
platform publishes.
