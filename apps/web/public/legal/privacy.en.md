# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

OpenMCP Hub (Tianjin Julians Technology Co., Ltd.) explains how personal information is collected, used, stored and protected when you register, sign in, browse, install, purchase or onboard as a creator. The full website Privacy Policy controls. We follow lawfulness, fairness and **data minimisation**; unused features do not trigger collection of their related data.

## 1. Information we collect

**You provide**

- Sign-in: email, phone, password hash; third-party login identifiers as authorised.
- Device Code auth: device/user codes, auth status, client id, linked account and security logs for client install flows (e.g. Store MCP / OpenWork).
- Creator KYC and payout: identity / business documents and payout channel identifiers when enabling paid listing.
- Orders: order id, asset, amount, status; **we do not store payment passwords or bank card numbers**.

**Generated automatically**

- Login and security logs (IP, device/browser, timestamps).
- Browse, install, invocation and usage logs for delivery, metering assistance, rate limits, risk control and debugging.
- Sandbox inputs/outputs if you use previews.

## 2. Purposes

Account auth, delivery and entitlements, order matching and dispute assistance, security, notices and legal duties. New purposes require fresh consent where required by law.

## 3. Storage and security

Stored in mainland China where required; kept only as long as needed; deleted or anonymised after account closure except where law requires retention. Encryption, access control and audit apply; KYC files are access-restricted.

## 4. Sharing

Shared only as needed with payment / identity verification partners, cloud storage, messaging providers and entrusted model providers for sandbox — never sold. Compulsory legal disclosures excepted.

## 5. Your rights

Access, correct, delete, withdraw consent and request account deletion via support@openmcp.cn after identity verification.

## 6. Minors

Services are intended for adults; minors need guardian consent.
