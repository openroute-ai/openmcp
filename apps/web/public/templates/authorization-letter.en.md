## Scope of the authorisation

The company above hereby authorises the grantee to act on its behalf on the
OpenMCP Hub platform (openmcp.cn) for the following onboarding steps:

1. submitting the company verification documents, including the business
   licence and the ID documents of the legal person;
2. confirming the payout channel details;
3. giving the confirmations required by the Platform Service Agreement and the
   Privacy Policy;
4. completing any follow-up updates to the account information after
   verification is approved.

## Term

This authorisation takes effect on the date it is signed and ends at the
earlier of:

- the moment the verification review is completed;
- the moment the company notifies the platform in writing that it is revoked.

## Limitations

1. The grantee may only carry out the steps listed above and nothing else.
2. This authorisation is neither transferable nor re-delegable.
3. A document signed by the grantee within this scope is treated as signed by
   the company itself.
4. The company guarantees that the information it provides is true, accurate
   and complete, and accepts responsibility for anything that is not.

## General

1. This authorisation takes effect once signed by the legal person and stamped
   with the company seal.
2. Anything not covered here is governed by the Platform Service Agreement.
3. Disputes arising from this letter are submitted to the court with
   jurisdiction over the place of registration of the company.
